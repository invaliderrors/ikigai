# Ikigai app screens

Scope: every screen of the macOS app (menu bar panel, Library, Studio, New Wallpaper + one-image wizard, Welcome, Settings). Visitor mode: Operate. Audience and job: PRODUCT.md and the confirmed app map (docs). Code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: You are the projectionist. Every display is a screen and every wallpaper is the feature showing on it. Refuses the category default of a dark Steam-style storefront grid with neon accents, and the bland grey utility.

OWN-WORLD: House-dark warm charcoal (#17120f) content, a deep seat-red sidebar (#3b1519 to #2a0f12), tungsten amber (#f2b544) for selection and the one primary action, screen-white (#f4efe6) text, exit-green (#4fae7a) for live, changeover red (#e0533d) for destructive. Big Shoulders Display, uppercase and tracked, only on letter boards and screen titles; SF Pro for everything else. Native macOS controls and layout throughout.

STORY: Players see at once what is showing on each of their screens, change it in one or two clicks, and trust that it pauses when nobody is watching. Creators go from one picture to a wallpaper playing on their real desktop.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Library window. Sidebar left (Play, Create, Discover), the display strip across the top of the content (each screen at true aspect, casting its wallpaper's light under a letter-board label), the thumbnail grid below, and an inspector right with a large preview and the tungsten "Show on All Screens" button.

FORM: Projection Booth, position 6 of 7 on my ordered list (assigned). seed:91a0df51fbd1bc0d. Raises: Studio construction grid (menu bar, Dock, notch zones); one decision at a time in Welcome and the wizard; one type size in inspector details; hover previews on every screen in the strip.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
